Official Institutional Academic Rules & Guidelines

Mandatory operational code of conduct for Students, Faculty Teachers, and Administrators.

✨ Creator: Manas Kumar
🏛️ 1. Classroom Session Capacity Limit

Each individual classroom room supports up to 40 Students and 2 Teachers concurrently. Multiple concurrent classes (e.g. MATH-101, PHYS-202) can be scheduled and hosted simultaneously by different teachers (Google Meet architecture).

🚨 2. AI Proctoring & Anti-Cheating Rules

Active camera frame presence is required for students. Tab switching away from the exam/lecture is monitored (3 warnings max). Automated evidence snapshots are timestamped and permanently logged in the institutional audit trail.

📧 3. Candidate Registration & OTP Policy

Student and Teacher registration is unlimited. All registrations require a verified 6-digit email OTP (valid for 10 minutes). Only authenticated candidates may enter institutional virtual classrooms.

🎙️ 4. Mic/Cam Controls & Admission Lobby

Students knock to request classroom entry and wait in the lobby until the host Teacher approves. Both teachers and students have self-controls for mic and camera, while teachers retain remote mute/video overrides.

📚 5. Homework & Assignment Submissions

Students can submit and resubmit homework coursework anytime prior to the due date. Teachers review submissions, calculate attendance scores, and generate automated AI performance reports.

🛡️ 6. Executive Super Admin Governance

Executive Managers hold institutional override authority: instant bypass user provisioning, global password overrides, real-time live classroom termination, and institution-wide emergency broadcast capability.
